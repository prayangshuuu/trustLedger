The problem

In Bangladesh, 43% of adults have a financial account: 33% of women and 54% of men. 70% borrowed money last year, mostly informally. Banks decide on bank statements and salary slips, which garment workers, gig workers, students and homemakers do not have.

Moon's story

Moon is 24 and works in a garment factory in Ashulia. She has no bank account and no salary slip, but she pays every electricity bill on time, recharges her phone regularly and sends money home every month.

  1. She grants consent per data source in Bangla, and can revoke it any time.
  2. Her wallet SMS are turned into 10 habit features on the phone; her DESCO bill photo is read by DeepSeek-OCR.
  3. Five agents score her: 691 (Good), P(repay) 0.93, approved.
  4. She sees why in Bangla: “বিল সময়মতো পরিশোধ”, “নিয়মিত মোবাইল রিচার্জ”; and one tip: “প্রতি মাসে আয়ের অন্তত ৫% সঞ্চয় করুন।”
  5. Three on-time installments raise her limit by 20%.

No history at all? Bhorsha, a 10–12 question Bangla conversation, gives a first score from financial-planning behaviour, with consistency and anti-gaming checks.

Gender, region, division and age are never model inputs; they only choose the group threshold and feed the audits.

Fairness and inclusion

Privacy

  • Consent per source (SMS, bills, recharge, photos, Bhorsha); revoke any time.
  • Raw SMS stays on the phone; only 10 derived numbers are stored.
  • Bill photos encrypted, deleted after 30 days; never in the database.
  • Presidio removes phone numbers and IDs before any AI call; logs are scrubbed.
  • “Delete my data” erases every record and image.

Full details: docs/privacy.md.
