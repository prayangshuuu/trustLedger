# Bangladesh Bank Guidelines on Digital Nano Lending

**Issuing Authority:** Banking Regulation and Policy Department (BRPD), Bangladesh Bank  
**Circular Reference:** BRPD Circular No. 11/2022  
**Source URL:** https://www.bb.org.bd/en/index.php/mediaroom/circular  

## 1. Scope and Eligibility of Digital Nano Loans
Bangladesh Bank has approved commercial banks and licensed non-bank financial institutions (NBFIs) to disburse digital nano loans to unbanked, financially excluded individuals and low-income borrowers using automated credit scoring algorithms and mobile financial services (MFS).

- **Maximum Loan Ceiling:** A single borrower may receive up to BDT 50,000 (Fifty Thousand Bangladeshi Taka) as a digital microloan.
- **Tenure:** Short-term repayment tenures between 1 month and 6 months, structured into weekly or monthly installments.
- **Interest Rate Cap:** Standard central bank interest cap applies (maximum 9% per annum or as revised by market-based reference rate SMART guidelines). Hidden processing fees, usurious service charges, or predatory penalties are strictly prohibited.

## 2. Automated Underwriting & Alternative Credit Scoring
Lenders must utilize transparent, explainable alternative credit assessment models.
- **Alternative Data Sources:** MFS wallet transaction frequency, utility bill payment punctuality, mobile airtime consistency, and psychometric assessments may be evaluated.
- **Fairness & Non-Discrimination:** Automated algorithms must not discriminate based on gender, religion, ethnicity, or geographical origin. Disparate impact across demographic groups must be actively monitored and mitigated.
- **Explainability:** When a digital loan application is declined, the automated platform must communicate clear, actionable reasons in Bengali to the applicant explaining why the decision was made.

## 3. Borrower Consent and Data Sovereignty
No customer data (transaction histories, SMS, utility records) may be harvested or evaluated without explicit, granular, revocable consent.
- Third-party sharing of borrower phone data is prohibited.
- Borrowers possess the right to delete their processed data and revoke active consents at any time.
