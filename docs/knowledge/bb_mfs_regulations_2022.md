# Bangladesh Bank Mobile Financial Services (MFS) Regulations

**Issuing Authority:** Payment Systems Department (PSD), Bangladesh Bank  
**Circular Reference:** PSD Circular No. 04/2022  
**Source URL:** https://www.bb.org.bd/mediaroom/circulars/psd/apr182022psd04.pdf  

## 1. Transaction Limits and Account Categorization
Under the updated MFS regulatory framework, individual personal accounts are governed by tiered transaction thresholds to promote financial inclusion while ensuring AML/CFT compliance:
- **Cash In Limits:** Daily maximum BDT 30,000 across maximum 5 transactions; Monthly maximum BDT 200,000 across 25 transactions.
- **Send Money / P2P Transfer Limits:** Daily limit BDT 25,000; Monthly limit BDT 200,000 across maximum 50 transactions.
- **Merchant Payment & Bill Pay:** Utility bills, educational fees, and approved merchant payments are exempt from strict personal caps to encourage digital payments.
- **Maximum Balance Ceiling:** An individual personal MFS wallet may hold up to BDT 300,000 at any given time.

## 2. Consumer Protection & Dispute Resolution
- **Failed Transactions:** In the event of a network timeout or debit without beneficiary credit, reversal must be executed within 24 to 72 business hours.
- **Fraud Reporting:** 24/7 dedicated customer helplines (e.g., 16247 for bKash, 16167 for Nagad) must provide instantaneous account freezing upon borrower request.
- **Interoperability:** Binimoy interoperable payment system facilitates seamless fund movement between banks, NBFIs, and MFS operators without punitive fee structures.
